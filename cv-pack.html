The method

Six steps. Thirty minutes. Every application.

The guide walks you through each step with examples, so tailoring becomes a routine instead of a struggle.

schedule5 min
1

Read the job like a recruiter

Spot the must-haves and repeated words, and write one sentence: "They want someone who can ___."

schedule5 min
2

Build a keyword map

Match 6–10 key phrases from the job description to your real proof.

schedule5 min
3

Rewrite your title and summary

Put the employer's top requirement in your very first sentence.

schedule10 min
4

Reorder your bullet points

Lead with your most relevant wins in the employer's own language. Relevance beats volume.

schedule3 min
5

Adjust your skills

Use the exact tool names they use, and cut what doesn't help.

schedule2 min
6

Check and save

Right company name, working links, a clean PDF and a professional file name.
