No. The 3-Month UI/UX Design Bootcamp is completely beginner-friendly and starts from fundamental design concepts.

Applicants must already possess basic UI/UX design skills and fundamental HTML/CSS concepts. If you are starting fresh, completing our baseline UI/UX Bootcamp or taking the All-Access Bundle is recommended.

Graduates and alumni of Jun Academy automatically receive a ₦50,000 discount, paying ₦100,000 instead of ₦150,000. Simply select the Alumni track option when completing your enrollment.

Yes! All sessions are conducted live online via Google Meet. Class recordings are provided so you can revisit lessons anytime.
